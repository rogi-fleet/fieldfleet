Multi-Zone Classification
Each affected unit/zone may have a different category and class — assign independently based on conditions found, not assumed from source
Sprinkler Discharge
Always treated as Cat 1 at source; however, water passing through ceiling voids, ductwork, or contaminated materials must be re-classified at point of contact
